Last Call, by Bike

Montréal’s bike-share data shows a city that commutes like clockwork by day. After dark it tells a different story, down to the minute the bars close.

Reporting, analysis, design, code and writing. 23.7 million BIXI trips, January 2025 to August 2026, processed in Python and DuckDB. Scroll-driven maps, campus small multiples and charts built in D3.

Montréal · Interactive

The Chains Aren’t Eating Montréal. They’re Moving.

Fourteen years of city food-inspection records show chains losing ground downtown, spreading into the suburbs, and doing no measurable harm to the independents next door.

Reporting, analysis, design, code and writing. Built a fourteen-year history of every food business in the city from inspection records, then compared closures next to 673 new chain locations with closures a few blocks away. Sixteen borough maps on one timeline, in D3.

Furniture · Interactive 3-D

A Folding Backgammon Board

A permanent board in cast concrete and blackened steel, played with shells picked up on the beach.

I never understood why every board is made of wood. I wanted a permanent board: not one you carry to the game, but one you go to. Its proportions come from the body: open, it is four-thirds of an average pair of shoulders wide and one arm’s reach long. There is no hinge: one half lifts by its strap and lays face-down on the other, where eight magnets hold it shut.

Furniture · Interactive 3-D

The Right Angled Rocker

A rocking chair pressed from one sheet of stainless steel, with a soft seat grooved for the body and a back you sink into.

From the side it is an L of three 45 cm squares, every angle a right angle. But the base is one long, shallow arc, so it rocks, and everywhere your body touches is soft: a channel under each thigh, and a cupped back with a swell for the small of your back.
