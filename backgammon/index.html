A Folding Backgammon Board

A permanent board in cast concrete and machined metal, played with shells picked up on the beach.

Designed by Zachary D. Lewin · Interactive 3-D model · 2026

I love backgammon, and I never understood why every board is made of wood. I wanted a permanent board: not one you carry to the game, but one you go to yourself.

So the frame is cast concrete, and almost everything else is mechanical: blackened steel, brass, flush magnets. The checkers are the exception. They are shells, picked up on the beach. To me the contrast between the two is the point: what we build, and what we already have. It is the same feeling I get from the eco-brutalist architecture of the Barbican in London, where raw concrete and growing things share the same space.
